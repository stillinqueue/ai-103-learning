Teams Notification Policy

Urgency rules:

Critical:
- Complete service outage
- Security incident affecting active systems
- Major business process unavailable

High:
- Important service degraded
- Significant user impact
- Business process partially unavailable

Medium:
- Limited user impact
- Workaround available
- Non-critical service degradation

Low:
- Informational update
- Planned maintenance
- No immediate user impact

Notification rules:
- Never invent a cause.
- Never invent a resolution.
- Never invent an ETA.
- Only recommend an action if the source explicitly provides one.
- If no next action is supplied, use "Await further information."
